i How to read the map

What can I explore?

Start with a phenomenon to find ways to study it. Or choose Observation or Simulations in the Field menu, pick a technique, and follow it to an astronomy question. Some topics also have an interactive study.

What makes a star large?

Ideas that help explain more of the map have larger stars closer to the center. An idea gets more credit for topics that build directly on it than for topics several steps away.

Where does a line lead?

Follow a dashed line to see how the same diagram can help explain two topics. Select the line to compare them.

Further reading

Technique pages link to the observatories and researchers who explain how the methods work. For background on the astronomy topics, see the textbook below.
