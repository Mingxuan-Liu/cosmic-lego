# Cosmic Lego — brick-built galaxy concept

Created 2026-11-02 as a new logo candidate following the owner's request for the
tactile brick-relief style of the supplied LEGO Milky Way wall-art reference.

Asset: [cosmic-lego-galaxy.png](cosmic-lego-galaxy.png).

## Design choice

A spiral galaxy expresses the assembly idea through the entire celestial object:
many interlocking pieces form a coherent structure. Its subject is broad enough
to represent astronomy beyond the initial exoplanet demonstration. The raised
ivory/gold core and two sweeping blue/teal arms supply a recognizable silhouette;
coral, violet, and magenta pieces connect those larger color regions. Open space
between the arms prevents the relief from becoming a solid, crowded disk.

The supplied product photo guides materials and layered construction. The new
mark uses an isolated galaxy rather than the reference's framed rectangular
mosaic. It contains no lettering, corporate logo, or product packaging. The
galaxy is an artistic branding metaphor, not an observation or numerical model.
