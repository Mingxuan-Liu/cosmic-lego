What this model means

Independent Keplerian orbits around a fixed star. Eccentricity and orbital orientation belong to each planet. Planets do not interact gravitationally.

The stellar disk is uniformly bright; opaque foreground planets reduce the measured flux. Camera movement changes only your view. Observer direction changes the measurement.

All signals are model predictions. Body markers are enlarged in the world view; orbital distances remain linear.
